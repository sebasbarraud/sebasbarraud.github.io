I've spent 17+ years moving between screens and printing plates — 12 of them focused on UX/UI, shaping interfaces for platforms in retail, healthcare, government and B2B. The rest is illustration, motion, branding and screenprint work that keeps the instincts sharp.

My process leans on research, prototyping and usability testing as much as it leans on typography and composition. I care about interfaces being both well-reasoned and well-made — accessible, on-brand, and genuinely easy to use.
